## How a source is ingested

1. Agree on its exact scope (which folders, which period).
2. Every item of the source is listed and counted in a ledger: the source is in when none is left, never on an impression.
3. Three trial notes, read by {{NAME}}: format and tone are settled there, not after a hundred notes.
4. The batch, as `seed`, each note with a `source` pointing to the original. Each item ends as a note, or is set aside with a reason {{NAME}} has seen.
5. Lint, build, five notes drawn at random and read together.
6. The ledger closes the source. Only then: save, and tick the box here.
