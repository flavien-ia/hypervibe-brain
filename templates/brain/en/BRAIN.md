## The trust model

The machine **creates** freely: what it writes enters as `seed`, with its source. On a validated note it **only adds**: it never rewrites {{NAME}}'s text, it may add a fact that says where it comes from and keep its own machine zones up to date, and on {{NAME}}'s thinking (a validated concept, idea, decision or lesson) it adds nothing and proposes the change in the weekly letter. Each write is in the brain's journal and is undone alone. {{NAME}} exercises their veto at the weekly review.

A seed is a note the machine wrote and nobody has validated yet: to be read with care. It becomes trusted when {{NAME}} validates it (in the live view, the "Seeds" filter shows them and each has a "Validate" button; or by telling Claude which note is right), or when the weekly review finds it has taken root.
